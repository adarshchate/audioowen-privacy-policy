Privacy Policy

App: AUDIOOWEN Music Player
Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AUDIOOWEN Music Player ("AUDIOOWEN", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application.

AUDIOOWEN is designed to let users discover and play audio and video files available on their devices. The app also uses Google AdMob to display banner and app-open advertisements.

By using AUDIOOWEN, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law or platform policy, we will request consent as appropriate.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, AUDIOOWEN may access the following information:

  • Media files: Audio and video files stored on your device that are made available to the app.
  • Media metadata: Information associated with media files, such as file names, titles, artists, albums, durations, and other available media details.
  • App preferences: Settings and preferences needed to support the app's features.
  • Playback information: Certain local playback state or library information needed to provide playback and related functionality.

The information accessed depends on the Android permissions granted, your device, and the features you use.

2. Media Permissions and Local Files

AUDIOOWEN may request Android permissions to discover, display, and play supported media files on your device. The permissions requested depend on your Android version and the app's features.

Your media files are intended to be played from their available storage locations. The app does not need to upload your music collection to a personal AUDIOOWEN server for ordinary local playback. However, third-party services and SDKs may process certain technical information as described in this policy.

You can manage or revoke app permissions through your device's Android settings. Revoking media access may prevent some app features from working correctly.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

AUDIOOWEN may store app settings, library information, and playback-related data locally to support its functionality. The information stored depends on the features you use.

You may be able to remove local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. These actions do not necessarily remove information that has already been processed by third-party services.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

AUDIOOWEN uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and app-open advertisements. These ads help support the development and maintenance of the application.

Google and its advertising SDKs may collect or process information such as the following, depending on the SDK configuration, applicable permissions, your consent choices, and local law:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device information and identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • App interactions, ad impressions, and advertising performance information.
  • Diagnostics, crash information, and other technical data.
  • Consent and privacy preference signals used for advertising.

The exact information processed depends on Google's services, the SDK version, your device settings, and the applicable advertising and privacy requirements.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Help Center
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy guidance

5. How Information May Be Used

Information accessed by the app or processed by its service providers may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing media discovery and playback features.
  • Remembering app preferences and supporting the user experience.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Diagnosing errors and improving app reliability.
  • Protecting the app and its services against abuse.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. The app's use of third-party services, including Google AdMob, may involve information being processed by those providers for advertising, diagnostics, security, and related purposes.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect rights, safety, or the security of the app and its users.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies, service terms, configurations, and applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Media files remain in their existing device storage locations unless you move, modify, or delete them. AUDIOOWEN may retain certain app preferences or playback-related information locally while it is needed to provide the app's features.

Local information may be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the application. Data processed independently by third-party services is subject to their retention and deletion practices.

If you have a privacy question or request concerning information associated with AUDIOOWEN, contact us using the email address provided below.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, such as requesting access, correction, or deletion.

You can also:

  • Review advertising and privacy settings available on your device.

Some information may be retained or processed where required for legitimate legal, security, or operational purposes.
